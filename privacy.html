Privacy Policy - Schedlix

1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy explains how Schedlix handles your information and protects your privacy.

Effective Date: July 9, 2026.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect or store your personal data on our own servers. Your work shifts, notes, settings, and other app data are stored locally on your device. Third-party services used for subscription management and advertising (such as RevenueCat and Google AdMob) may process only the technical information necessary to provide their services in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses RevenueCat, Apple App Store, and Google Play to process and manage subscriptions. These services may process technical information required to complete purchases and verify subscriptions in accordance with their own privacy policies.

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Advertising

The free version of the app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect certain technical information, including advertising identifiers, IP address, device identifiers, and similar technical information to serve and measure personalized ads based on your interests and your use of this app or other apps and websites.

Depending on your region (for example, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you will be asked for consent before personalized advertisements are displayed, and you can manage your preferences at any time.

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data .

5. Data Deletion

Deleting the app removes all locally stored app data from your device. If your data has been included in an operating system backup (such as iCloud Backup or Google Drive), it may be restored if that backup is restored.

6. Cloud Backups

If you choose to use iCloud Backup, your app data is stored in your personal iCloud account. We do not have access to this data. Storage and processing of iCloud backups are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes become effective immediately upon publication on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at schedlix.app@gmail.com.
